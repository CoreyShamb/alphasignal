# AlphaSignal Product Vision

## Product Name

AlphaSignal

## Tagline

AI-Powered Stock Intelligence & Market Research Platform

## Mission

Help investors transform market data into actionable intelligence through AI-powered analysis, company research, news aggregation, and educational resources.

## Target Users

- Beginner Investors
- Retail Traders
- Long-Term Investors
- Financial Researchers
- Students

## Core Value Proposition

Data + Intelligence + Education

AlphaSignal combines market data, company intelligence, AI analysis, and learning resources into a single platform.
